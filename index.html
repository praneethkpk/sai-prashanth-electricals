FAQ

Common Questions in Hyderabad

Do you take TSSPDCL permissions for pole / transformer / meter work?

Yes. We prepare estimates, drawings & applications and coordinate with TSSPDCL section/division offices for approval, inspection and charging.

What is the cost of transformer or pole erection?

Depends on KVA, HT/LT length & site conditions. Share your load & location via the quote form for a free site inspection and written estimate. See transformer page.

Do you do complete apartment electrification?

Yes — from sanctioned load to flat meters, rising mains, panels, earthing & common-area lighting. Staged billing linked to slabs. See builders page.

How do I choose the right electrical contractor in Hyderabad?

Pick a licensed team with TSSPDCL liaison experience, written BOQ billing, branded switchgear and test reports on handover — not just the lowest quote. We offer free site inspection with transparent estimates. See our services.

How much does an electrical contractor charge in Hyderabad?

It depends on scope (HT/LT, poles, transformer KVA, panels, wiring points) and site conditions. We inspect free and give a written estimate within 24 hours — request your quote.

Which areas do you cover?

All Hyderabad & Secunderabad: LB Nagar, Dilsukhnagar, Uppal, Tarnaka, Kompally, Miyapur, Kukatpally, Kondapur, Gachibowli, Old City, BN Reddy, Hastinapuram + outskirts. Full list: service areas.
