Cost of Transformer Installation in Hyderabad

Transformer cost = DTR price (25–500 KVA) + plinth/structure + HT/LT cabling + earthing + TSSPDCL development charges. For apartments: 20 flats ≈ 100–200 KVA; 50 flats ≈ 250–315 KVA (after diversity + lifts).

Process

  • Load calculation → KVA freezing
  • TSSPDCL sanction + estimate payment
  • Yard plinth, fencing, earthing
  • DTR supply, testing, charging
